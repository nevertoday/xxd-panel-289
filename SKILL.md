## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one small folk mark, not a scene and not a mascot. Keep one core shape, an exaggerated identifying feature and a rounded imperfect contour. Use dry gouache, crayon grain and a little risograph error on warm paper. Color is a soft limited palette taken from the photograph. Type is a tiny collection label. Reject a commercial logo, a standard mascot, smooth vector, 3D and piled ornament.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
